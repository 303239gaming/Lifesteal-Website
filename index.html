Questions

FAQ

Common questions about the server and crates.

What version is the server? ▼
The server runs Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11. Make sure you're on that version before connecting.
How do I receive my crate after buying? ▼
After purchasing, join the server and contact an admin with your payment confirmation. They will give you your crate key in-game to use at the spawn arena.
What is Lifesteal? ▼
Lifesteal is a PvP gamemode where killing another player steals one of their hearts and gives it to you. Lose all your hearts and you're eliminated. Max hearts on this server is 20.
Are crates pay-to-win? ▼
Crates give you a head start with gear and resources, but skill is what wins fights. Good PvP players will always have the upper hand regardless of crate items.
Can I refund a crate? ▼
All crate purchases are final once the key has been delivered in-game. If you have an issue before receiving your key, contact us through Discord.
